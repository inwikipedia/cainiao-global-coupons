Frequently asked questions

What to know before using a code

How do I use these promo codes?

Each of the 9 destinations has its own code. Copy the matching code, paste it into the “Promo Code” field during Cainiao consolidated-shipping checkout, and the shipping discount is applied automatically.

Where do I enter the Cainiao promo code?

Look for the “Promo Code” or coupon field in the international shipping checkout flow and paste the code for your destination.

Can I combine the 15% discount with other coupons?

The checkout page shows which offers stack with the code and any discount cap — follow the on-screen prompts to apply every available discount.
